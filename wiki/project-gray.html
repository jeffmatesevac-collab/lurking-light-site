Project Gray (primer)

Premise

A gray-skied fantasy where color has to be earned back. That's the whole public pitch, and it's staying that way for a while.

What's canon

  • The sky is gray.
  • Color is not gone. It's somewhere.
  • Buster from Skip & Buster is not in the moon in this universe. Probably.
