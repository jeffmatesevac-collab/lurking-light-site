Glow & ranks

Glow

Glow is site-wide XP. You earn it by visiting parts of the site, RSVPing to Friday Hang, posting, and giving sparks. The pill in the nav shows your rank and progress.

Ranks

RankGlowFlavor
Lurker0watching from the rocks
Drifter40floating closer
Glimmer100you've started to glow
Beacon200people find the lobby by you
Lightbearer350you hold the lure up

Sparks

Sparks are how you say "this was good." You get 10 a day; keep your daily streak going and they refill.

The catch

Right now all of this lives in your browser. Real accounts (and real, keep-forever glow) come with the Account page later.
