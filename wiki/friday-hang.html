Friday Hang

What it is

Friday Hang is the one standing night. Voice is open, games rotate, and nobody has to stay. It's how most people end up in the guild: they show up once, stay for "one more run," and keep coming back.

How to show up

  1. Join the Discord (link in the nav and footer).
  2. Hop into the hang voice channel when it's live.
  3. Say hi or don't. Lurking is a valid lifestyle.

What we play

Whatever the room wants: WoW (Forever or Retail), co-op chaos games, a tabletop one-shot, or nothing at all while someone shows off a build.
