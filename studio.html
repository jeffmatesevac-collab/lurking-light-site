Lurking Light is a small production studio run by the same people who hang out in the guild.

No pitch deck voice here: we like weird little worlds, characters with teeth, and the kind of games you tell your friends about at 2 a.m. We build in public with the people in the lobby, and we start small on purpose. Lurker is our mascot. He's friendly. Mostly.

How we work

  1. Start with something people use. A guild tool or an addon earns trust before a whole game does.
  2. Build in public. Builds land in Discord first; the lobby breaks them.
  3. Keep it small. Finish little things. Let the big things grow out of them.
